# Cited boss notes

Hero resource loops (Winded, Blind/Ruin, Pain, Combo reach) live in `cited-classes.md`. Same stack.

A rule ships only if it comes from this stack, in order:

1. Game CSVs / in-game data - the fact
2. [Official wiki](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/) Strategy - the intent
3. Grand Slam / Infernal writeups - only when the wiki is thin and they agree
4. Our logs - only to check we implemented the cited rule

Do not encode a fallback that only we invented.

## Dreaming General

- **Sources:** CSV `lost_battalion_taproot`, `lost_battalion_dreaming general`, token / effect / condition / buff / dots exports; [wiki Strategy & Advice](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Dreaming_General#Strategy_&_Advice) (its 5-round tap routine is superseded by the CSV facts below).
- **CSV facts:**
  - Taproot: `m_IsHealthless`, resists everything, `m_DeathChainIds` General (dies with him). Round start: `taproot_start_growth` (round 1, everyone `tangle_a`), then `taproot_growth` on up to 2 heroes without `tangle_c` each round: token replace a -> b -> c. `tangle_b`: speed -2 and a 2 HP/turn strangle DoT. `tangle_c`: the hero's only legal skill is `taproot_tangle_c` (turn lost, 75% 1 stress to each ally), and the strangle DoT stacks every turn.
  - Hitting the Taproot (`taproot_damage_listener`): +1 `taproot` token (limit 3, consume manual). The 2nd token gives it `taproot_assign_targets` (2 heroes get `taproot_target`). `taproot_damage_untangle_listener`: if any hero has a b/c vine, an extra `taproot_retreating_growth` turns one of them back to `tangle_a` and removes the strangle DoTs (logs: the retracted hero is random among b/c, not the locked one first).
  - `dreaming_general_waking_dead` (forced): needs `dreaming_general_taproot_ready` (3 `taproot` tokens) and `target_has_taproot_target`; 10-20 dmg + `skill_dot_large_bleed` (4 x 3) + 2 stress on both targets, then clears the tokens. Only hero hits on the Taproot arm it.
  - `dreaming_general_nightmare`: 10-20 on every `tangle_c` hero, resets them to `tangle_a`. `dreaming_general_whisper`: 6-10 + 2 stress, 1 stress 50% to the others.
  - General: 185 HP, 3 `deaths_door_armor`. At 0 HP every damage instance (DoT ticks too) eats one token instead of killing (`ActorInstance.CalculateWillHaveDeathsDoor`); the hit after the last token kills.
- **Rules encoded:**
  - Never make the hit that gives the Taproot its 3rd token (arms Waking Dead).
  - Below that, tap only when a hero is locked (`tangle_c`) and no hero has `tangle_b` (the random retract must free the lock). One tap per locked hero. So at most two taps a fight.
  - Otherwise defer Taproot and hit the General. A hero with no connecting Taproot click hits the General even while a tap is wanted (no idling behind a deferred boss).
  - Never use `taproot_tangle_*` hero skills if a real skill is legal.
  - Taproot is healthless: preview damage is always 0. A connecting attack still counts as the wiki "when Hit" tap. Do not walk to reach Taproot — if this hero cannot tap it, hit the General.
  - Tracking Shot / Blinding Gas on Taproot is a wasted Combo mark. Never pick it; use a connecting swing if one is legal, otherwise hit the General.
  - Healthless fixtures do not count as killable enemies. A last-General kill (or a slap on Death Armor at 0 HP) is allowed even if Taproot is still up and someone is on Death's Door.
- **Not encoded:** the wiki's scheduled cadence (R1 none, even rounds one tap, odd rounds one per b/c hero) and "slap Taproot whenever anyone has vine B/C". Both arm Waking Dead every third tap; CSV says vines also reset via Nightmare at a lower cost. Spending the two free taps to dodge a Nightmare on Death's Door heroes (the random retract and the delayed reset make the sign unclear).
- **Nightmare timing (logs):** the General casts Nightmare exactly when every living hero holds `tangle_c` (`m_MultiHitAllTargetsConditionIds target_has_taproot_tangle_c`); otherwise Whisper. Nightmare is the vine reset, so a tap that does not free a locked hero only delays it and keeps the others locked longer. Only Highwayman / Grave Robber connect with the Taproot from the starter party's ranks.
- **What loses this fight (logs):** a lock cascade. Stress is the engine: a locked turn is 75% 1 stress to each ally, Whisper 2 + 50% 1, so heroes overstress (mostly Meltdowns, HP to 10%), more heroes get locked, and a full lock brings a Nightmare onto heroes already on Death's Door. Arrival HP and stress matter.

## Shackles of Denial

- **Sources:** [wiki Strategy](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Shackles_of_Denial#Strategy) (Health first or second-to-last; then the lock that denies most of your damage; GR/HWM teams the Ranged lock, Leper/Hellion the Melee lock; Stress last); [r/darkestdungeon 19348th](https://www.reddit.com/r/darkestdungeon/comments/19348th/tips_to_fight_shackles_of_denial/) (focus one; primary damage type; Stress last; do not finish Health beside a wounded lock). CSV `boss_brain lock {health,melee,ranged,stress}`, `skill_block`, `token`, `buff`, `effect`, `condition`, `battle_configuration`, `overstress` exports.
- **CSV mechanics:**
  - 4 locks, 55 HP (61 in some runs), speed Health 1 / Stress 2 / Melee 3 / Ranged 5, one turn each per round. `battle_configuration` `mountain_boss_brain`, `_2`.._`_5` only shuffle the ranks: Melee is always rank 1-2, Health 1-4, Ranged 2-4. `m_RunDataStatsId,no_retreat`; no round limit, no torch drain per round (the only `torch_triggers` row is hero death); the fight ends when all four die.
  - Every round one lock's `brain_lock_*_activate` (start_round, `m_Cooldown,2`, ignores Dodge/Guard/Stealth/Blind) puts `lock_*` (1 round) on every hero: `skill_block` Melee = skills tagged `melee`, Ranged = `ranged`, Health = `heal` + health_heal effects, Stress = `stress_heal`. It also gives that lock `logic_temporary` -> forced unique: Melee 10-13 on ranks 1-2 + Weak, Ranged 6-10 on ranks 3-4 + Blind, Health 8-11 + a large bleed/blight/burn, Stress 8-11 + 3 stress (+25% 1). `clear_skill_cooldowns_if_2_endboss`: at 2 locks or fewer an activation every round. Logs: exactly one activation per round.
  - The other locks use Crippling Doubt (4-6 on 2 heroes, `stress_damage_1` + 25% 1) or Paralyzing Fear (3-6, 75% Stun while 3+ heroes, Vulnerable). Logged damage per use: Doubt 9.5 / 2.0 stress, Fear 4.2, Melee unique 23.1, Ranged 14.7, Stress 9.8 + 2.8 stress, Health 8.8 + DoT.
  - Death buffs on the survivors (permanent): Melee +20% damage, Ranged +10% crit, Stress 10% Blind / 10% Weak on hit. Health: `health_lock_death_heal` (33% HP) needs `brain_health_lock_is_dead` and `performer_has_0_count`; it and `count_plus_1` run from `health_lock_death_buff_manager`, which sits on the three **other** locks (their `spawn_effects`). The `count` tokens land on those holders (logs: after Melee died, Ranged and Stress carried `count`, Health never did), so the heal fires on **every** Health death, not only the first lock death. Logs: every Health death that left a wounded lock behind healed it (Health 2nd: Ranged 8 -> 26; Health 3rd: Stress 38 -> 55 and 45 -> 55).
  - Overstress at 10: 80% Meltdown (`meltdown_health` HP down to 10%, stress to 1, -15% deathblow resist until the next inn), 20% Resolute (heal 67%, stress 0).
  - Damage race: a dead lock stops attacking (logged incoming per round: 32.1 with 4 locks, 27.4 with 3, 20.5 with 2), and the locks never heal except the Health death heal. The starter party removed 8.4-13 lock HP a round in logged attempts (`KitSafety.PartyRoundDamage` uses 12 to estimate how many DoT ticks a large target lives through).
- **Rules encoded:**
  - Kill order. If this party's equipped attacks are more `ranged` than `melee` (the starter party is): Ranged, Melee, Health, Stress. Ranged dies first (the mildest death buff); Health second-to-last (wiki) heals only Stress, which is whole unless splash / ripostes reached it. Otherwise Health first while all four live (wiki option A; Melee's +20% would land on three locks), then Melee, Ranged, Stress; if a lock already died, Health goes second-to-last.
  - Per hero: the `must_kill` is the first lock in that order this hero damages with a legal attack this turn (a lock token or the rank often blocks the head of the order). The others are deferred but open. Nothing reachable: the head stays the must-kill (rank walks).
  - Stress (Shackle of Despair) last.
  - Meltdown guard. CSV: every lock's `brain_lock_*_doubt` is `stress_damage_1` + `stress_damage_1_25pct` on two heroes; `brain_lock_stress_unique` is `stress_damage_3`. Overstress `meltdown` (80%) is `meltdown_health` = HP down to 10%. So a hero at 8+ stress here is one Doubt from Meltdown; in the logs Meltdowns were the main HP loss in this fight. A legal Bolster-type stress heal on that hero beats a non-kill swing / Retribution (`meltdown_guard`), and is not hit by the party-crisis support penalty.
  - Any fight: `pass_stress` is `stress_damage_2` on the performer. At 8+ stress it is a self-Meltdown, so it scores below every other click (a swap included). Logs: every such pass checked against the next record overstressed.
  - **Which lock attack (`denial_ev`, `DenialPlay.BestLockAttack`).** CSV `dots_data_export`: a DoT lasts 3 of the lock's turns (`skill_very_large_blight_dot` 5 x 3, `skill_medium_bleed_dot` 3 x 3) and DoTs stack (logs: a Health lock ticking 8 = 3 + 5); locks never cleanse. Every lock has to die, and a dead lock stops attacking (above). So one damage point on the lock in place p of the kill order (n living) is worth n - p (it brings the deaths of locks p..n forward). Value of a lock attack = sum over the locks it hits of weight x (direct damage clamped to the bar + DoT x land x 3 ticks clamped to what is left after the hit and the pending tick) x hit chance, +6 x weight for a kill. When the default scorer's pick is a lock attack (gates `attack`, `attack_over_idle`, `open_add`; no cited note, no rank walk, not a kill), the best-valued legal lock attack replaces it if it is worth 3 more; never a click that moves the performer, an item, a cursed skill or a self-hit. The kit-safety notes `dot_host` and `kit_blind` are score terms here, not cited picks (`DenialPlay.ScoreOnlyNotes`): otherwise a `dot_host` Plague Grenade kept Magnesium Rain from ever being weighed. Health's death heal (fires on every Health death, above): (a) while a lock the order kills before Health still lives, damage that takes Health below 15 (pending DoT included) is worth nothing; (b) a click that kills Health pays the weighted HP the heal gives every other living lock (min(33% of its bar, HP missing after this click's own hits)), and such a kill no longer stands on its own — it is weighed like any other click (a kill with every other lock whole stands); (c) while Health lives, damage on a lock the order kills after it (Stress for the starter party, every other lock for a Health-first party) counts only past the first 33% of its bar missing, the part the heal gives back. Why: the default scorer pays the per-hero must-kill +120 and a deferred lock -75, vetoes a deferred-lock hit while the must-kill is reachable, and pays a DoT 1.5 ticks (docked when stacking), so it took Duelist's Advance (walks the HWM, 5) over Wicked Slice (8.7), Incision over Noxious Blast (its very large blight, 5 x 3), Hand of Light over Judgement. Golden cases: `denial_rain_over_grenade_round_one`, `denial_ev_blinded_grave_robber`, `denial_heal_band_no_stress_splash`, `denial_judgement_burn_full_ticks`.
  - **Walk back onto a launch rank (`denial_walk`, `DenialPlay.WalkToLaunch`).** CSV `quirk_cowardice_neg`: turn start `move_to_back_if_in_rank_1_15pct`. Logs: the MAA went from rank 1 to rank 4 between turns with no skill moving him; every equipped MAA attack launches from ranks 1-3 at best (Crush 1-2, Rampart 1-3), and he then spent his turns on Bolster / Retribution / Defender / Rest / pass and never hit. A hero (not on Death's Door) with no damaging click on any lock whose rank launches none of his equipped attacks swaps one rank toward the nearest launch rank instead of an uncited support or a pass (Rampart then walks him forward itself); the swap partner must launch something from his rank. Crisis heals, `meltdown_guard`, `tank_riposte`, `cover_guard` and other cited picks stand.
  - **Free DoT items on a lock (`denial_dot_item`, `DenialPlay.ScoreDotItems`).** CSV `items_combat skill_data_export`: every combat item is `m_IsFreeAction`; `spiked_ball` `skill_dot_very_large_bleed` (5 x 3), `bear_trap` `skill_dot_medium_bleed` (3 x 3), `crows_feet` `skill_dot_very_small_bleed` (1 x 3) on every enemy; locks resist bleed 10% and never cleanse. `ItemPolicy.ScoreAttack` prices an attack item on direct damage only (8 + 0 + 4 = 10-14 < `UseThreshold` 18), so in the logs a held Crow's Feet / Spiked Ball / Bear Trap was never used here. A free DoT item on a lock worth at least 5 lock HP (DoT x land x 3 ticks, clamped to the bar and the heal band above) scores 20 + weighted value / living locks and is used before the hero's action, on the lock where it is worth most (crisis heal items still score higher). Golden cases: `denial_spiked_ball_free_dot`, `denial_crows_feet_free_dot`. Not encoded: `toxic_ichor` (resist debuffs only), `thunderclap_grenade` (`move_shuffle` on the locks, 25% stun).
  - **Multi-target DoTs on the locks (Magnesium Rain, Plague Grenade, Flashing Daggers p3).** `denial_ev` values every lock a click hits with that lock's own DoT and resist (per-hit preview, `notes/cited-classes.md` DoT host): CSV `pd_magnesium_rain` burn 3 x 3 on every rank against burn resist 0.1 / 0.1 / 0.5 / 0.2 is 8.1 + 8.1 + 4.5 + 7.2 = 27.9 lock HP on four fresh locks (weighted: Ranged 4, Melee 3, Health 2; Stress inside Health's heal band 0 = 65.7) against Noxious Blast's 56 on the head lock, so it is the round-1 click when legal. Its `target_team_effects clear_corpse` no longer keeps it out of `denial_ev` (only a corpse click is).
- **Not encoded (the logs cannot separate it):** Health last instead of Stress last now that the heal always fires (Health alone activates every round, `lock_health` blocks every heal skill each round; Stress alone blocks only stress heals); Melee first for the reach it gives all four heroes vs the +20% Melee death buff on three locks (with all four locks alive heroes reach Melee most often and Ranged / Stress least, so the Ranged-first order splits the party: back heroes on Ranged, front heroes / PD on Melee); bracing ranks 1-2 (Defender / heal) in a round whose start activated Melee (its unique hits ranks 1-2 for 10-13 each). Holding HP heals off a hero at 8-9 stress (Meltdown takes HP down to 10% anyway) — the Death's Door case cuts the other way (-15% deathblow resist). Stun resist / Clarifying Poultice inn prep (wiki). Round-1 `meltdown_guard` spending the front hero's two Strength tokens on a 1-stress Bolster.
- **What decides it (logs):** hero damage per hit and arrival state. The fight is a damage race; parties that arrive at full HP with low stress and heroes whose trinkets, quirks and mastery raise their damage on the locks win it, and a hero arriving at 8-9 stress (often from the mountain cultist fight, Cultists section) usually melts within the first rounds. Deaths come on Death's Door, from Doubt / Fear / a unique, mostly after a stress reset.

## Tangle / Lost Battalion mashes

- **Sources:** [The Tangle](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Tangle) - Bishops resurrect via Benediction; Drummers grant Order and move-resist, “killing the Drummer is a high priority.” CSV: Bishop `serve_once_more`; Drummer `death_before_dishonor`.
- **Rules encoded:** Bishop `must_kill` first; Drummer `commander` next. Knight / foot / arbalist deferred while either is a connecting legal target. Guard redirect onto a foot is not a Drummer hit. A 0-damage Combo mark on a deferred add is skipped while the Drummer / Bishop is up. Controller flags share one predicate with add-marking so a mash Drummer is not tagged add.

## Harvest Child (Foetor lair)

- **Sources:** [wiki Strategy](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Harvest_Child#Strategy_&_Advice). CSV/classes: `plague_eater_harvest_table` (Child), `*_fetid_meat`, `*_putrid_meat`. Hunger token `harvest_hunger`; rank 1 is forced `harvest_hunger` / Feed the Hunger (-15% max HP, unresistable).
- **Rules encoded:**
  - Focus the table. Both meats are deferred while the table is alive. Do not finish a meat if the table is a legal attack.
  - Rank 1 with `harvest_hunger` is forced Feed the Hunger (CSV `harvest_hunger`, `target_is_meat`). That click is not skipped as a deferred meat punch or a crisis-add punch — the game has no other skill.
  - If another hero has Harvest Hunger and this hero does not, prefer MAA Hold the Line / Hellion Toe to Toe (wiki: take rank 1 and immobilize so the hungry hero is not pulled into the eat). Those skills are not vetoed just because they hit a meat.
  - If this hero is hungry, those same forward-moves are penalized (they walk you into rank 1).
- **Not encoded:** inn prep (Apples and Cheese, antivenom). Immobilize on the meats themselves (Leper Bash / net) is not wired.

## Relationship curses

- **Sources:** CSV `skill_modifier_data_export` - negative relationships set `m_IsForceEquip` on the cursed skill (`hateful_a`, `suspicious_a`, `envious_a`, `resentful_*`). Using it applies the rider to the partner (Vulnerable, Taunt, stress, Blind/Weak).
- **Rules encoded:** if a cursed attack/support is legal and a non-cursed attack is also legal, the cursed skill is penalized (-40). Crisis heals are never penalized. If the cursed skill is the only hit on the focus target, it still wins.

## Healer rank

- **Sources:** Battlefield Medicine `launch_ranks` 3,4; `m_Limit` 3. Hero `*_move` is adjacent swap (`m_IsMoveToTarget`, relative -1/+1).
- **Rules encoded:** if an ally is in crisis (Death's Door or <=35%) and no skill heal is legal, allow a move that lands the healer on a launch rank of an equipped heal that still has uses. Do not un-veto every swap. An enemy attack with a self-heal rider (Crush on Combo) is not a skill heal — do not walk for it.
- Cleanse items that also heal (antivenom, bandage) are spent on Death's Door / <=30% instead of being scored as a wasted cleanse.
- If the skill heal is spent (`m_Limit`) and someone is on Death's Door or <=30%, food/bandage/antivenom that still heal are forced. A last-enemy kill does not skip a Death's Door heal. Support (Ounce of Prevention) is penalized while the party is in crisis.
- Last living enemy behind corpses, or a must-kill this hero cannot damage (Librarian after Categorize): if no damaging attack is legal on that target, walk onto a launch rank of an equipped attack that can hit that rank. 0-damage Combo marks do not count as a hit and are not a skill to walk for. `pass_heal` is Rest, not a skill heal — do not walk for it. Do not un-veto every swap. Skip that walk when an ally already hits the tile from their current rank (MAA/Dismas ping-pong on Altar). Librarian: the walk fires even when an ally reaches him, but never with a move that slides that ally off his reach.
- Combat items are classified from CSV conditions/tags, not a name list: `target_is_corpse_hidden` (Pouch of Lye), `target_has_*_dot` (DoT cleanse), `target_is_diseased` (Single Leech), `target_has_blind` (Rag), `stress_heal`, `heal`. Enemy items stay attacks (witchbane). Grenades that click a corpse still skip. Hero clear skills (`lep_purge` `clear_corpse`) use the same `corpse_reach` boost when a front corpse blocks living targets. Buffs (powders, war horn, The Blood) stay on the token/buff scorer — do not dump them.

## Chirurgeon (Gaunt table)

- **Sources:** [wiki Strategy](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Chirurgeon#Strategy). CSV: `shared_lost_soul_chirurgeon`, `shared_lost_soul_patient`, `_widow`, `_yeoman`. Leucotomy heals 33% and buffs patients each round. Boss-node modifier otherwise marks every gaunt as a boss.
- **Rules encoded:**
  - While the Chirurgeon is up, he is `must_kill`. Patients / Widow / Yeoman (and other lost-soul / gaunt packmates) are deferred adds; their boss flag is cleared.
  - Wave 1 with no Chirurgeon is unchanged (Yeoman still scores as large support).
  - Appalling Apron (CSV `chirurgeon_appalling_apron_01` turn start `hot_heal_small_if_dmgdot`, `_02` turn end `remove_all_bleed/blight/burn`; wiki: one tick, then Regeneration, then the DoTs are gone). A DoT attack on him ranks just below a legal non-DoT hit on him that does at least as much direct damage. A kill is never demoted. Logs: two Poison Darts gave him a HoT that lifted him off Death's Door twice (`deaths_door_exit_effects` re-adds `deaths_door_armor`).
  - A legal kill on him beats a crisis heal unless the heal target is urgent (Death's Door, dying to DoT, <=15%). He leaves Death's Door on any heal and gets his armor back.
- **Not encoded:** inn prep, DoT-then-Cause-of-Death, Trepanation negative-token bait.

## Librarian (Sprawl lair)

- **Sources:** [wiki Strategy](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Librarian#Strategy). CSV: `fanatic_librarian` / `_ignited`, stacks `fanatic_librarian_stack_l` / `_m` / `_s`. Page Burner `librarian_books_destroyed` extra action; Ignite when no books left.
- **Rules encoded:**
  - Focus the Librarian. Book stacks are deferred while he is up, including when he is out of reach — support/pass rather than punch a stack.
  - Do not finish a stack. Wiki: destroying books lets him Ignite sooner and grants a free party-wide Burning Bright. Kill him before the stacks are gone (about 6-7 rounds of his own Page Burner).
  - A self-heal rider on an enemy attack (Crush on Combo) is still an attack, so MAA can actually hit him.
  - AoE that splashes a stack (Flashing Daggers) is penalized the same as clicking the stack.
  - A 0-damage Combo mark is not a hit. If this hero cannot damage him from this rank, walk onto a launch rank of a damaging attack (Pistol, Chop, Judgement). Do not restack Combo. If a damaging attack on him is already legal, take it over Tracking Shot.
  - One reach walk per round for the party. Do not move an ally who already reaches him off his reach (Categorize ping-pong). Out of reach after that: support/pass, not another walk.
  - Categorize walk (wiki: "Your damage dealers need to reach every rank"; Categorize "sorts the party by English class name, so ... bring repositioning skills"). A hero with no damaging click on him walks even when an ally already reaches him, as long as the move leaves every ally who reaches him in reach. A move puts the hero on the target's rank and slides everyone between one step back (`*_move` `m_TargetRelativeRanks -2,-1,1,2`, `m_IsMoveToTarget`; logs: MAA rank 4 -> 2 over HWM / GR gave HWM, MAA, GR, PD). Same score as other reach walks (180, 90 in a party crisis), so crisis heals still win.
- **CSV mechanics (`fanatic_librarian*`, `battle_configuration city_dungeon_3_a..d`, effect / condition / buff exports):** Librarian 116 HP, speed 5, **3 turns a round**, 2 `deaths_door_armor`, burn resist 1 (immune), bleed 0.5, blight 0.3, stun 0.4, move always resisted. Rank 4 behind three 15 HP stacks (`_3_a` L M M, `_3_b` M L M, `_3_d` L M S); stacks resist everything but burn (0.1). `librarian_count_manager`: +1 `count` every round start. With count 1 his skill is Page Burner (stack directly in front: L -> M -> S -> destroyed; destroying an S gives +3 count and an extra turn, then count -1) plus 1 (+50% 1) stress on a random hero; with 3 count Burning Bright (all heroes 3-6 + small burn, +1 Dodge, clears count); with 0 count Mandatory Reading (4-8 + small burn + 1 stress, no cooldown), Smokestack (cooldown 2: 2 Blind + Vulnerable on a hero, +1 Dodge), Categorize (cooldown 8, launch ranks 3-4, `sort_by_type_class`: logs always GR, HWM, MAA, PD), Backdraft from rank 1 (ranks 1-2, burn, +2 Dodge, back 3). No books left (`librarian_books_destroyed`): Ignite -> `fanatic_librarian_ignited` (140 HP, `MAX_HP_DELTA` = +24 HP, speed 8, 2 turns a round, Conflagration every turn: all heroes 3-6 + small burn). `librarian_crit_burn`: his crits add a medium burn. Dodge / Blind tokens are 50% evade, removed on the roll.
- **More CSV / IL mechanics:**
  - The clock is fixed by the layout. Page Burner (`m_IsFriendly`, `m_TargetRelativeRanks -1`, needs `target_is_librarian_books` + 1 `count`) only burns the stack directly in front of him; `librarian_convert_stack_l_m` / `_m_s` change its class, `librarian_destroy_stack_s` kills an S (+3 `count`, extra turn: the free Burning Bright). Count +1 every round start, so one burn a round: L = 3 burns, M = 2, S = 1. `_3_a` L M M and `_3_b` M L M = 7 burns -> Ignite round 7; `_3_c` S M L and `_3_d` L M S = 6 -> round 6 (logs: every finished fight). Ignite (`librarian_ignite_self`) needs `librarian_books_destroyed` = no `book_stack`-tagged monster left. A stack the party destroys is one or more burns he no longer needs (Ignite sooner) and gives no count; a damaged stack still takes the same burns (Page Burner converts the class, HP plays no part).
  - He starts on rank 4 and only moves up as the stack in front dies: rank 4 for the first 2 rounds (`_3_a` / `_3_b`), rank 3 for 2-3 rounds, rank 2 for the last 3 (`_3_a`) or 2 (`_3_b`). From rank 4 only Pistol Shot and Thrown Dagger (launch 2-4, target 2-4) reach him; Crush (target 1-3) from rank 3; Wicked Slice, Pick, Noxious Blast (target 1-2) only from rank 2. `_3_b` keeps him on rank 3 a round longer than `_3_a`.
  - Stacks: `m_IsBattleComplete` True, move resist 0.1, immune to everything but burn. The game's end check (IL `NoTeamBattleCondition.IsConditionMet`) counts only actors without `m_IsBattleComplete`, so killing him ends the fight with stacks up. Knockback works on a stack (90%); he resists move always. A stack knocked behind him is burned last (Page Burner looks only in front), so the number of burns, and Ignite, do not change; on rank 1 with no stack in front his only skill is Backdraft (launch rank 1, ranks 1-2, +2 Dodge, `move_backward_3`), which puts a stack back in front. All his count-0 skills (Mandatory Reading, Smokestack, Categorize) launch from ranks 2-4 only.
  - Stun resist 0.4 and `turn_skip_effects stun_temp_resist_buff_e`; a stun costs him one of 3 turns, not the Page Burner (count stays 1 for the next turn). Ignited: speed 8, 2 turns, Conflagration every turn (3-6 + small burn on all heroes), HP = HP at Ignite + 24 (`MAX_HP_DELTA`), 2 `deaths_door_armor` again (+1 `add_1_deaths_door_infernal_flame` with the stagecoach deathblow upgrade).
- **What decides it (logs):** a race against the Ignite clock.   - Ignited (`librarian_race`): a legal damaging attack on him replaces any other pick (crisis heals, Rest, guard, support) except a free-action item. CSV: two party-wide Conflagrations a round, no retreat, no other way out.
- **Not encoded:** knockback of a stack behind him (CSV above: no change to the clock; the gain is reach for Noxious / Wicked Slice / Pick a round or two earlier at the cost of a hit on him; unverified whether the swap passes a move-immune actor); stun (one of 3 turns, temp resist after); Blind via Blinding Gas (already scored as a peel); ignite-phase item / Burn Salve / Ablative Powder inn prep (wiki); a free offensive item to eat a Dodge (wiki); PD Plague Grenade (launch 3-4, target 3-4) in the Librarian loadout (the loadout is the player's choice).

  - Deacon or Cardinal on the board: while Altar is alive it is the exclusive `must_kill` (Cherub / Evangelist deferred; Herald stays legal for splash). Boss deferred. Exemplar is deferred in those cases; Cherub / Evangelist stay deferred. Herald stays legal (not deferred) while Altar is the must-kill so splash can still chip. When Exemplar HP% ≤ ~25% and Altar is gone (or Worship is 0), Exemplar is `must_kill` and soft adds are deferred; Herald remains legal but not forced. Worship stacks on Exemplar also escalate Altar/Herald focus pay. Skip a deferred punch only when this hero has a damaging must-kill click (HP, or a Taproot tap). Blind Gas / Tracking Shot / any Combo-only tap is not that click — Pillar is Exemplar's forced skill, Blind on Altar does not stop it. If this hero cannot damage Altar, walk onto a launch rank; if an ally already reaches it, a real swing on the deferred target is allowed. On Altar, a bigger HP swing beats a higher-score DoT open unless Altar already dies to DoT.
  - Holy Water / Combo-strip on a hero who has Combo: spend it — PickAction hard-gate (same utility rule as Implication Blind: not over a kill / last-bar / party crisis). Wiki: The Fall on Combo is how Exemplar gains Worship for Exultation. Same strip gate applies on Ravenous Reach p1 (Setback).

If Combo is live in ranks 1-3 and this hero cannot strip it, walk onto rank 4 when an equipped Taunt skill launches from there (not Hold the Line / Toe to Toe — those walk you forward). Do not Fall-walk while Altar is the must-kill (Pillar is worse than The Fall, and the walk spends the party's one reach-walk). Retribution does not open over a damaging Altar hit.

- Deacon/Cardinal deferral is priority only. The boss and the deferred Cherub / Evangelist are `OpenWhenUnreached`: a hero with no damaging click on the must-kill swings at them instead of a 0-damage mark (Tracking Shot / Blinding Gas), a base-score support (Take Aim, Ounce at +2), a 0-HP heal (Artemisia) or a pass. A DoT open counts as a hit. Logs: a Highwayman / Plague Doctor who could not reach Altar (rank 4) or Cherub (rank 3) otherwise spent about half their turns idle while Wicked Slice / Incision on the Deacon (the only damage dealer) was legal.
- **Mountain cultist fight (`cultist_mountain_*`, last fight before the mountain boss, no inn between):** `battle_configuration` `cultist_mountain_1_1`..`_1_7` (Deacon + two of Cherub / Herald / Evangelist / Altar, Denial runs) and `_2_1`..`_2_5` (Cardinal), all `no_retreat`. Taunt token: `m_ConsumeTypes,forced_target`; Retribution `add_2_taunt_nr` = the next two single-target hits.
  - **Logs:** the Deacon deals most of the stress in this fight (Weight of Worlds and Sundering Steel); he is usually the last enemy alive. Heroes often arrive at the boss at 8-9 stress from here. Retribution's Taunt funnels the Deacon's single-target Sundering Steel / Exultation onto the tank: MAA ripostes return about a Crush, so it neither speeds nor slows the kill — Taunt moves stress onto the tank, it does not remove it. Bolster was often legal on a 5+ hero on MAA turns that took a swing instead.

## Must-kill finish over a non-urgent heal

- A sure kill (preview kills, hit >= 90%) on the `must_kill` beats a crisis heal unless the heal target is urgent (Death's Door, dies to DoT, <= 15% HP). Same shape as the Chirurgeon kill-over-heal. Logs (`lost_battalion_mash_*`): a Bishop at 1 HP from round 2 lived to round 12 and raised the Arbalist twice after a Grave Robber at 10/35 took Artemisia over a 100% Poison Dart kill.

## Meltdown guard over a weak swing (any fight)

- CSV overstress at 10 is 80% `meltdown` (`meltdown_health`: HP to 10%). A legal stress heal (Bolster) on an ally at 9+ stress (`MeltdownImminent`; 8+ in Denial) that already outscores the best attack is taken (`meltdown_guard`) instead of being overridden by `attack_over_idle`; not over a kill / last-bar finish (`ShouldOpenUtility`). Logs: allies left at 9 stress that way usually reset within 2 rounds, mostly Meltdowns. Bolster heals only 1, so this only fires where the swing was already the weaker click.
- **Stress policy (logs):** most stress resets are Meltdowns, with Dreaming General and Denial the largest share. Most Overstresses hit exactly 10, so one point of stress heal before the last hit would have moved that one. The last hit was often a hero's own click: Rest (`pass_heal` +2), `pass_stress`, the Taproot lock. Shipped: Rest at 8+ off Death's Door is not a heal (`RestIntoMeltdown`, `notes/cited-classes.md`).
- **Not encoded:** Bolster at 7-8 over a swing (taking it or not made no difference in the logs; only at 9+ in non-boss fights is there a gap, which `meltdown_guard` already covers when the swing is weaker; in Dreaming General and Denial every 9+ ally melted anyway). Retribution over a higher-scoring Bolster on a 9+ ally (the tank Riposte gate): those allies melted less often than the base rate, and Retribution's Taunt pulls the hits off them. Laudanum (CSV `stress_heal_1`, free action) is rarely legal on a 7+ ally; the gap is inn stock, not fight policy.

## How to add the next boss

Copy the wiki Strategy paragraph into this file, quote the CSV keys that back the numbers, then encode only those sentences. If a live log looks drunk, write the mismatch here - do not invent a new rule from the log.

Confession mountain bosses are all cited (Denial, Resentment/Seething Sigh, Obsession/Focused Fault, Ambition/Ravenous Reach, Cowardice/Body of Work) plus the Act 3+ Exemplar gate. Shambler has a CSV note (above). Other roaming (Collector, Death, Antiquarian, Warlord) and Kingdoms (Meat Hook, Mother of Threads, Archduke) have no extra targeting note — they play as default boss/support scoring unless a wiki Strategy sentence appears later.
